## Summary

Hemispheres is a research framework for studying multi-hop factual reasoning in transformer language models. It synthesizes controlled 'worlds' of entities and relations, trains GPT-style models (including a 'latent' variant with a differentiable key-value retrieval store) to answer multi-hop questions, and evaluates how well models generalize, handle knowledge edits, and resist information leakage. The project includes benchmarking tools for training throughput and latent retrieval profiling, an evaluation pipeline measuring exact-match accuracy across hop counts and edit conditions, and a reporting system for comparing experimental runs.
