## Summary

Hemispheres is a research codebase that studies how transformer language models store and retrieve multi-hop factual knowledge, using a fully synthetic 'world' of entities and relations (persons, companies, cities, etc.) as a controlled testbed. It implements and compares several training arms: a standard dense GPT, a 'latent' GPT with a differentiable key-value fact store that can read facts at inference time, a 'lookup' arm that emits explicit retrieval traces, and a 'context' arm. The project provides tooling for synthetic world generation, training, evaluation (including knowledge-editing and leakage tests), throughput benchmarking, and reproducible reporting.
